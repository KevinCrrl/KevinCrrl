Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to all of my repositories, projects, and associated community spaces. As the creator and a member of these spaces, I share these guidelines solely so that everyone understands how to maintain order, harmony, and a welcoming environment for collaboration.

1. Inclusion and Diversity

Everyone is welcome here. No form of discrimination or belittling will be tolerated against any person based on their age, gender, country of origin, race, religion, sexual orientation or any other kind, personal tastes, or even their coding style, experience level, or choice of tools.

2. Collective Responsibility in Conflict

For this space to work, we must all stand together to prevent conflict. The health of the community is a shared responsibility. If a misunderstanding, difference of opinion, or conflict arises, it should be resolved as a community in an appropriate, mature, and public manner (for instance, within the same discussion thread or issue) through open dialogue and mutual respect.

3. Regarding Moderation Actions

Since I am just one person managing these projects in my spare time, I do not have the capacity to act as a judge or private mediator in disputes. Due to this strict lack of time, enforcement actions such as deleting comments or restricting access will be strictly reserved for repeated violations of these guidelines or extreme cases where public dialogue has completely broken down.

4. Contact

If you believe a situation requires direct attention or cannot be resolved openly, you can reach out to kevincrrl@tuta.io.
